# Website Summary

This website is a unified travel platform that combines a travel blog, a curated photo gallery, and a practical trip-planning tool in one editorial-style experience. It is designed to feel immersive, image-rich, elegant, and useful for both discovering destinations and organizing trips.

The homepage is structured around three main sections: suggested trips, completed trips, and region recommendations. Its role is to inspire visitors, showcase past journeys, and guide destination discovery through a premium travel-journal style rather than a generic booking or startup interface.

The planner page uses a split layout with one third of the screen dedicated to a simple flight table and two thirds dedicated to a price calendar. The goal of this page is to let users compare flight options quickly while also exploring the best travel dates visually.

The `/trips` page acts as the main archive of journeys. At the top, it offers a choice between **Trip Reports** and **Trip Plans**, followed by a list of trips linking to their individual pages. Each trip preview includes a title, regions, dates, a short description, and a few attached photos.

Each individual trip page is designed as a detailed travel log. It starts with a header summarizing the trip title, dates, main destination, and cities visited, then continues with one section per day. Every day contains a title, a written description, a list of activities, a small photo gallery, and a list of food eaten.

The gallery page is organized by trips, with each trip name displayed above a group of photo thumbnails. Clicking a thumbnail opens a full-size lightbox, and pill-style tags for regions and cities help users browse the visual archive by place.

The site also includes a **Prepare Your Trip** page, which combines travel preparation guidance into one long-form resource. This page brings together essential apps, visa or entry information, and payment setup instructions, with a sticky side navigation linking to each section.

Across the whole website, the design language is calm, spacious, sophisticated, and editorial. The site balances inspiration and utility, blending strong photography, elegant typography, and structured travel-planning features into one cohesive travel experience.
