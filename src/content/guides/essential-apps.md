> Download and set up these apps before your flight. Some need phone verification that does not work without a local SIM.

## Must-have

You use these every day.

### WeChat (<span lang="zh">微信</span>)

English available. If you download only one app, make it WeChat: messages, payment, ride-hailing and food orders in one app.

- Messages and video calls with anyone in China
- WeChat Pay: scan QR codes in shops, restaurants and at street stalls
- Mini-programs: use Didi, order food and buy train tickets with no extra app
- Share your live location with hotel staff or friends

Before you travel: register with your phone number, then link an international Visa or Mastercard to turn on WeChat Pay.

### Alipay (<span lang="zh">支付宝</span>)

English available. The most widely accepted payment app. You need it for metro QR codes, shared bikes and places that do not take WeChat Pay. Set it up before you arrive.

- Register with your passport. Visa and Mastercard are supported.
- Scan to pay at millions of shops
- Turn on the metro mini-program for each city to ride the subway
- "Tour Pass" mode for international visitors

### Amap / Gaode (<span lang="zh">高德地图</span>)

Partly in English. The map app that locals use. It is more accurate than Baidu Maps and has an English interface. On an iPhone, Apple Maps is a backup.

- English interface
- Accurate public transport, walking and driving routes
- Offline maps: download them before you lose signal underground
- Ride-hailing built in (no separate Didi needed)

### Didi (<span lang="zh">滴滴出行</span>)

English available. China's Uber. You do not negotiate fares or explain the destination in Chinese: type the address or drop a pin.

- Full English interface
- Pay with Alipay, WeChat Pay or an international card
- Also available as a WeChat mini-program (no download needed)
- Shows the estimated fare before you confirm

## Good to have

For train travel and to read Chinese text.

### 12306 (<span lang="zh">铁路12306</span>)

English available. The official China Railway app to book high-speed trains, now with a full English interface. Cheapest option, with no service fee.

- All routes and timetables, the most reliable source
- Register with your passport number
- E-tickets on your phone: scan them at the gate
- Tickets go on sale 15 days ahead at 14:00 Beijing time

### Trip.com / Ctrip (<span lang="zh">携程</span>)

English available. Books flights, trains and hotels in China. Train tickets from Trip.com are sometimes cancelled or the seats changed, so use 12306 for trains when you can.

- Hotels with free cancellation and English reviews
- Domestic flights between Chinese cities
- English customer service 24/7

### Pleco

English available. The best Chinese dictionary. Use it for menus, signs and basic conversations. It works fully offline.

- Offline dictionary
- Point the camera at Chinese text for instant translation
- Draw a character to look it up
- Core features are free

## Advanced

For longer stays or more adventurous travellers.

### Baidu Maps (<span lang="zh">百度地图</span>)

Partly in English. A reliable alternative for navigation. Google Maps hardly works in China; Baidu Maps knows every bus route, subway exit and side street.

- Accurate public transport with real-time bus and subway tracking
- Offline city maps: download them before you lose signal underground
- Find restaurants, ATMs and hospitals near you

### Meituan (<span lang="zh">美团</span>)

Chinese only. The app for local life: food delivery, restaurant reviews and attraction bookings. Very useful, but you must read Chinese.

- Order food to your hotel
- Read restaurant reviews and ratings
- Book attraction tickets at a discount

### Baidu Translate (<span lang="zh">百度翻译</span>)

English available, App Store only. A backup translation tool for live conversations, signs and menus.

- Camera translation for menus and signs
- Voice translation for conversations
- Conversation mode: speak English, it answers in Chinese
