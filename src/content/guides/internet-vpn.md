> Download your VPN before you arrive. You cannot download VPN apps from inside China.

## Quick decision

| Your trip | Best setup | Approx. cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Less than 10 days | Roaming or eSIM + VPN | $10–45 |
| More than 10 days | Local SIM + VPN | $25–60 per month |

## International roaming

Your current phone plan (for example Vodafone, Telstra or T-Mobile) works in China with no setup: turn on roaming when you land. It costs about 5–10 AUD per day. Some carriers even bypass the Great Firewall, because the traffic goes through your home country.

Best for trips under 10 days when you want no effort. For longer stays, an eSIM or a local SIM costs much less.

## Short trips (under 10 days)

Best option: eSIM + VPN. Buy it before you fly and activate it when you land. You need no physical SIM.

| Provider | Data | Price |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Airalo | 1–10 GB | About $5 per week. Choose the plan carefully: some do not bypass the firewall. |
| Trip.com eSIM | 5G, day pass or data packs | From about 0.32 AUD per day. May not open TikTok. |

Most eSIM plans do not include a firewall bypass. You still need a separate VPN app.

## Long trips (10 days or more)

Best option: local SIM card + VPN. Buy it in the airport arrival hall or in any China Mobile or China Unicom shop (bring your passport). Plans cost ¥59–128 per month for 10–40 GB.

A local SIM gives you a Chinese phone number. You need one for WeChat registration, shared bikes, food delivery apps and more.

## VPN comparison

You need a VPN for Google, WhatsApp, Instagram, YouTube and Facebook. Install at least 2: no VPN works 100% of the time.

| VPN | Reliability in China | Price per month | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| LetsVPN | ★★★★★ | About $3–5 | Short trips |
| Astrill | ★★★★★ | About $12.50 | Expats and frequent travellers |
| NordVPN | ★★★★ | About $3.99 | Low budget, obfuscated servers |
| Surfshark | ★★★ | About $2.49 | Low budget, many devices |
| ProtonVPN | ★★★ | About $4.99 | Privacy |

Based on traveller reports from r/travelchina and r/chinalife (February 2026).

> Airport Wi-Fi in China needs a Chinese phone number for SMS verification. Activate your eSIM or buy a SIM card as soon as you arrive.

## Tips

- Take screenshots of everything before you lose internet: hotel addresses, booking confirmations, maps.
- WeChat works without a VPN. Use it as your main way to communicate in China.
- Apple Maps works in China without a VPN (it uses local map data).
