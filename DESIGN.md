## Visual Theme
A warm, sophisticated editorial space that mimics high-end print travel quarterlies. Built on rich oatmeal tones, deep charcoal ink, and accented with a earthy, sun-baked terracotta.

## Art Direction
- Concept: A premium digital travel monograph where atmospheric photography meets high-fidelity planning tools.
- Signature move: A split-screen sticky planning panel that locks contextually during scroll.
- Layout rhythm: Spacious and highly structured, utilizing clear borders and asymmetric grid frames.

## Typography
- Scale: editorial
- Headlines: Cormorant Garamond — Elegant, high-contrast serif with classic print proportions.
- Body: Plus Jakarta Sans — Highly legible, modern geometric sans-serif for comfortable reading and clean data.

## Components
- Buttons: Sharp-cornered, subtle borders, with warm terracotta solid fills for primary CTAs. Text is always uppercase with slight letter spacing.
- Cards: Flat layout with very thin borders (1px HSL border), zero box shadows. Images inside cards have a very subtle warm color cast.
- Sections: Generous vertical padding (`py-20 lg:py-32`), separating sections with crisp horizontal divider lines rather than background color blocks.
- Imagery: High-grain, atmospheric photography with a natural, sun-drenched warm palette.

## Do's and Don'ts
- DO maintain generous white space to allow travel photography to breathe.
- DO use high-contrast serif typography for all major editorial statements.
- DON'T use bright neon accents, rounded bubbly cards, or generic startup-style illustration work.
- DON'T wrap display headlines inside narrow, cramped columns below 60% width.
