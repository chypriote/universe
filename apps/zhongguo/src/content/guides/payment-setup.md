## Alipay (recommended)

### Setup

1. Download Alipay from the App Store or Google Play.
2. Choose the international version and register with your passport.
3. Link a payment method: an international Visa or Mastercard.
4. Verify your identity: take a selfie and upload the photo page of your passport.

### Supported cards

Visa, Mastercard, Discover, Diners Club, JCB.

> Tell your bank that you travel to China before you link your card. Some banks block the first verification payment.

## WeChat Pay

### Setup

1. Download WeChat and create an account.
2. Go to **Me → Services → Wallet**.
3. **Add Card** and link your international card.
4. Verify your identity with your passport.

WeChat is also for messages, mini-programs and splitting bills with locals.

## Cash (backup)

Carry ¥500–1,000 in cash for old taxis, small towns and places that do not take mobile payment.

- Bank of China ATMs: best rates, inside bank branches
- Airport exchange counters: easy but bad rates
- Bank of China branch: best rates, passport required

## Where each one works

| Where | Alipay / WeChat Pay | International card (Visa/MC) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| International hotels | Yes | Yes |
| Airport shops | Yes | Yes |
| Large department stores | Yes | Yes |
| Restaurants and shops | Yes | Not small ones |
| Street stalls and markets | Yes | No |
| Didi and taxis | Yes | No |
| Metro | Yes | — |

Exchange rates change. Check the current rate before you travel.
