## Ordering food

### How to order

1. Walk in. In casual places you do not wait to be seated.
2. Look for a picture menu or a QR code on the table.
3. If the menu is on paper only, use camera translation on your phone.
4. Point at a dish and say "<span lang="zh">这个</span>" (zhè ge, "this one").
5. When you finish, say "<span lang="zh">买单</span>" (mǎi dān, "the bill, please") and pay.

Never tip. It is not part of the culture in China, and staff may run after you because they think you forgot your change.

## Dietary needs and allergies

> "Vegetarian" in China often still means oyster sauce, chicken stock or lard. Be specific: "<span lang="zh">不要任何肉和动物油</span>" (no meat or animal fat of any kind).

For a serious allergy, save the phrases below on your phone and show them to the staff before you order.

### Phrases

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <span lang="zh">我吃素</span> | Wǒ chī sù | I am vegetarian |
| <span lang="zh">不要肉</span> | Bú yào ròu | No meat |
| <span lang="zh">不要猪肉</span> | Bú yào zhūròu | No pork |
| <span lang="zh">不要牛肉</span> | Bú yào niúròu | No beef |
| <span lang="zh">清真食物</span> | Qīngzhēn shíwù | Halal food |
| <span lang="zh">我对…过敏</span> | Wǒ duì… guòmǐn | I am allergic to… |
| <span lang="zh">我对花生过敏，请不要放花生</span> | Wǒ duì huāshēng guòmǐn, qǐng bú yào fàng huāshēng | I am allergic to peanuts, no peanuts please |
| <span lang="zh">我对贝类过敏，请不要放贝类</span> | Wǒ duì bèi lèi guòmǐn, qǐng bú yào fàng bèi lèi | I am allergic to shellfish, no shellfish please |
| <span lang="zh">我对奶制品过敏，请不要放奶制品</span> | Wǒ duì nǎi zhìpǐn guòmǐn, qǐng bú yào fàng nǎi zhìpǐn | I am allergic to dairy, no dairy please |

Dishes and prices change from region to region.
