## Metro

### How to ride

1. **Buy a ticket** at the machine. The touch screen has English.
2. **Go through security.** Bags go through an X-ray scanner.
3. **Tap your token** at the gate.
4. **Find your platform.** Follow the line number and the direction.
5. **Exit:** put the token into the slot at the exit gate.

Skip the ticket machine: scan a QR code at the gate with the Alipay or WeChat metro mini-program. Contactless Visa and Mastercard also work in 28+ cities, Beijing and Shanghai included.

### Tips

- Rush hours are 7:30–9:30 and 17:00–19:00, and very crowded.
- On escalators, stand on the right and walk on the left.
- Each station shows the time of the last train. If you miss it, you need a taxi.
- Transfer stations can mean long walks. Give yourself extra time.
- Alipay transport is set up per city. Turn on the mini-program for each city you visit.

## High-speed rail (<span lang="zh">高铁</span>)

China has the largest high-speed rail network in the world. Trains go up to 350 km/h and connect all large cities.

### How to book

- **12306 app (official, recommended):** cheapest (no service fee), full English interface, register with your passport.
- **Trip.com / Ctrip:** full English interface, takes international cards. Less reliable: tickets can be cancelled or seats changed without notice.
- **Station ticket office:** bring your passport. Write the destination and date on paper for the clerk. Expect long queues.

Tickets go on sale 15 days ahead at 14:00 Beijing time. Popular routes sell out fast, mostly during holidays.

### Classes

- **Second class (<span lang="zh">二等座</span>):** the standard, and very comfortable. Power sockets and a lot of space.
- **First class (<span lang="zh">一等座</span>):** wider seats, more legroom, about 50% more expensive.
- **Business class (<span lang="zh">商务座</span>):** flat-bed seats with meals, 2–3 times more expensive.

Second class is excellent. You do not need to upgrade.

### At the station

1. **Arrive 30–60 minutes early.** Security and ticket checks take time.
2. **Bring your passport.** You need it to enter the station.
3. **Find your waiting hall.** The boards show the train number and the gate.
4. **Board:** scan your passport or the e-ticket QR code.
5. **Find your seat:** the car and seat numbers are on your ticket.

> Train stations work like airports. Security and passport checks can take 15–30 minutes.

## Taxis and ride-hailing

### Didi (<span lang="zh">滴滴</span>)

1. **Download Didi.** It has a full English interface.
2. **Link a payment method:** Alipay, WeChat Pay or an international card.
3. **Enter the destination** in English or Chinese, or drop a pin on the map.
4. **Choose the ride type:** Express (cheapest), Premier or Taxi.
5. **Confirm and wait.** The app shows the car and the number plate.
6. **Get in.** The driver already has the destination; no need to talk.
7. **Payment** goes through the app when you arrive.

In the car, the driver asks for the last 4 digits of your phone number to confirm it is you.

### Street taxis

- A green light on the roof means the taxi is free.
- Always ask for the meter: "<span lang="zh">打表</span>" (dǎ biǎo).
- Show the destination in Chinese characters on your phone.
- Carry small notes. Drivers often cannot change ¥100.
