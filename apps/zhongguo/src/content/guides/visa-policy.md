## Do I need a visa?

| Your situation | Visa needed? |
| --- | --- |
| My country is on the 52-country visa-free list | No, up to 30 days. See [visa-free entry](#visa-free-entry-52-countries). |
| I transit through China to another country | No, up to 240 hours (10 days). See [transit](#visa-free-transit). |
| I visit Hainan Island only | No, up to 30 days. See [Hainan](#hainan-island). |
| I visit Hong Kong or Macau only | No, for most nationalities. See [Hong Kong and Macau](#hong-kong-and-macau). |
| None of the above | Yes: tourist visa (L visa). See [tourist visa](#tourist-visa-l-visa). |

## Visa-free entry (52 countries)

China gives 30-day visa-free entry to holders of a valid ordinary passport from 52 countries, for business, tourism, visits to family or friends, exchange and transit.

New on 17 February 2026: Canada and the United Kingdom joined the program.

### Rules for visa-free entry

- Ordinary passport only. Travel documents and temporary or emergency passports are not eligible.
- Purposes: business, tourism, visits to family or friends, exchange, transit.
- Multiple entries are allowed. There is no limit on the number of entries or the total days.
- You can enter from any country (no third-country rule).
- You can arrive by air, sea or road.
- It does not cover work, study or news reporting.
- You cannot stay longer than 30 days unless you apply for a stay permit.

### How the 30 days are counted

The 30 days start on the day after entry and last 30 calendar days.

### Documents to carry

- An invitation letter, if you visit for business or to see friends or family
- Return or onward tickets
- Hotel bookings or other proof of accommodation

## Visa-free transit

China has two levels of visa-free transit for travellers on their way to another country.

### 24-hour transit (all countries)

Open to nationals of all countries at all open ports of entry.

- Hold a valid international travel document and a confirmed onward ticket.
- Stay no more than 24 hours.
- Stay in the restricted area of the port, unless immigration gives you a temporary entry permit.

### 240-hour transit (55 countries)

Nationals of 55 countries can stay up to 240 hours (10 days) when they transit through China to a third country or region.

1. **Book your travel.** You need an onward ticket to a third country or region.
2. **Enter through a designated port.** There are 65 designated ports in 24 provinces and regions.
3. **Stay in the permitted area** for your port of entry.
4. **Permitted activities:** travel, business, visits, family reunions.
5. **Leave on time,** within 240 hours (10 days).

### Eligible countries (55)

- **Europe (40):** Albania, Austria, Belarus, Belgium, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Bulgaria, Croatia, Cyprus, Czech Republic, Denmark, Estonia, Finland, France, Germany, Greece, Hungary, Iceland, Ireland, Italy, Latvia, Lithuania, Luxembourg, Malta, Monaco, Montenegro, Netherlands, North Macedonia, Norway, Poland, Portugal, Romania, Russia, Serbia, Slovakia, Slovenia, Spain, Sweden, Switzerland, Ukraine, United Kingdom
- **North America (2):** Canada, United States
- **South America (4):** Argentina, Brazil, Chile, Mexico
- **Oceania (2):** Australia, New Zealand
- **Asia (6):** Brunei, Indonesia, Japan, Qatar, South Korea, United Arab Emirates

### Rules for transit

- You must travel on to a third country or region, not back to where you came from.
- Allowed: travel, business, visits, family reunions.
- Work, study and news reporting need prior approval and the correct visa.
- Stay in the designated area of your port of entry.

## Special regions

### Hainan Island

Hainan has its own 30-day visa-free policy for tourists from 59 countries.

- Book through a registered travel agency in Hainan.
- The stay is for Hainan Island only, with no travel to the mainland.
- Your passport must be valid for 6 months or more.

### Hong Kong and Macau

Hong Kong and Macau are Special Administrative Regions (SARs) with their own immigration rules.

- Most nationalities can enter visa-free for 7 to 180 days, depending on nationality.
- A Hong Kong or Macau visa does not give entry to mainland China.
- SAR to mainland: you need a separate visa, or you must qualify for visa-free entry or transit.
- Mainland to SAR: you leave mainland China. To come back, you need a valid visa or must qualify for visa-free entry.

## Tourist visa (L visa)

If no visa-free option applies, apply for a tourist visa (L visa) at your local Chinese embassy, consulate or visa application centre.

### When you need it

- Your country is not on the 52-country visa-free list.
- You want to stay longer than 30 days.
- You come for work, study or news reporting (these need other visa types).

### Documents

| Document | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Passport | Valid for at least the length of the stay, with at least 2 blank pages |
| Application form | Completed and signed (download it from the visa centre website) |
| Passport photo | Recent, 48 × 33 mm, white background |
| Flight booking | Return or onward flight confirmation |
| Hotel booking | Accommodation for the full stay, or an invitation letter |
| Proof of funds | Bank statement for the last 3 months |
| Invitation letter | If you visit friends or family (include the host's ID and your relationship) |

### Steps

1. **Collect the documents** in the table above.
2. **Fill in the application** online at visaforchina.cn, or download a paper form.
3. **Book an appointment** at your nearest visa centre.
4. **Submit in person** with all originals and photocopies.
5. **Pay the fee.** It depends on your nationality and the processing speed.
6. **Collect your passport,** or get it by mail.

### Processing times

| Speed | Time | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Regular | 4–5 business days | Standard |
| Express | 2–3 business days | Extra fee |
| Rush | 1 business day | Limited, highest fee |

### Common mistakes

- Using a travel document or emergency passport instead of an ordinary passport
- Blurry photos or photos of the wrong size
- Blank fields on the form
- Booking flights and hotels only after approval (submit the bookings with the application)
- Forgetting to sign the form
